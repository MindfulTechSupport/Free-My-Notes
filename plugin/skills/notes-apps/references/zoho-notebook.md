# Zoho Notebook

## How Claude gets the notes
The backup.

## The full backup
1. In Zoho Notebook, open **Settings** and choose the option to export or download all your Notebook data.
2. Pick **HTML** (Claude can read it) rather than ZNote (Zoho's own format, for moving back into Zoho Notebook).
3. Start the export. Zoho emails a download link to the account's address when it's ready.
4. Keep the download in a backup folder, plus a second copy.

Source: Zoho Notebook help, "Export Options in Zoho Notebook" and "Export all your notecards from Notebook."

## What's in it
Every notecard as HTML, organized by notebook. Exports are a snapshot: anything added after the export starts isn't in it.

## Notebooks are called
Notebooks, holding notecards.

## Canceling
Notebook itself is free. Only Zoho paid plans the user no longer needs are worth canceling, in their Zoho account.

## Watch out for
- People without a registered Zoho account can't export everything at once; they export notecards one at a time.
- Zoho can't export a single notebook as one file; use the full export.
- Password-protected notecards stay locked until unlocked in the app.
