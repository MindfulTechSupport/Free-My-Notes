# Zoom notes (My Notes, meeting notes, AI summaries)

## How Claude gets the notes
- **Zoom's connector** (if the user's Zoom account allows it): it can read meeting summaries, transcripts, and Zoom Docs. Try this first; it avoids downloading meeting by meeting.
- **Or downloads, one meeting at a time.**

## The full backup (downloads)
In the Zoom Workplace app (or zoom.us in a browser):
- **A note:** open it from **Notes** → **More (⋯)** → **Export** → PDF. Some users also get a Markdown download.
- **A transcript:** open the meeting in the Hub → **Transcript** → the download icon (a .txt file).
- **A meeting summary:** open the meeting → summary → export or copy.
Save everything into one backup folder, named by meeting date, plus a second copy.

Sources: Guideflow "How to export a note in Zoom"; Zoom Community, "Need help exporting transcript of My Notes" (Apr–Aug 2026).

## What's in it
PDFs, Markdown, and text files, one meeting at a time. There is no export-everything button.

## Notebooks are called
Meetings (and Docs folders). Treat each month, or each recurring meeting, as a notebook.

## Canceling
Most people keep Zoom for meetings, so there's usually nothing to cancel. If notes were the only reason for a paid add-on, cancel it in the Zoom account's billing page.

## Watch out for
- In July 2026 some users reported the transcript download button disappeared from the Workplace app. If it's missing, use the connector, or The Great Escape's Autopilot.
- Meeting notes can hold other people's words. Ask before copying shared meetings into a shared place.
