# Privacy

*Free My Notes · Mindful Tech Projects · last updated 2026-10-27 (version 1.0.0)*

**The plugin doesn't send your information to Mindful Tech Projects.** It has no servers and no analytics. Everything happens inside your own Claude account and the apps you connect.

- **Your notes** are read through a connector you add and sign in to yourself (Evernote, Notion, Zoom), or from an export file you share, and copied only to the places you choose: files you download, your Google Drive, your Notion, and so on.
- **Sensitive notes** (passwords, account numbers, ID numbers, private legal, medical, or family notes) are never put into a Claude Project, Claude's memory, a progress log, or a shared folder. You decide what happens to each one. Copies the plugin makes for you can be deleted when you say so.
- **You're asked first.** Before saving any progress, the plugin tells you what it will remember and asks permission. If you say no, it works in the moment without saving.
- **What's saved (the progress log):** your name, which notes app(s), the buckets you chose, your destination, what you paid each month if you share it, notebook names and note counts, note titles and what you decided for each (titles of sensitive notes can be replaced with a number), and which steps are done. Never note contents or passwords.
- **Where it's saved:** in your own Claude account's memory, or a file in your own folder. Only you and your Claude can see it.
- **See it or erase it anytime:** "what do you know about me" shows it; "forget my escape" erases it. Erasing the log never touches your notes, your copies, or your backup.
- **Nothing is deleted from your notes app by the plugin.** Deleting notes or your account is your own step.
- **The backup service** runs as a scheduled task in your own Claude account and saves backups to your own Google Drive (or a folder on your computer), in a folder only you can open. Its settings file holds which app, the schedule, and the last run, never note contents or passwords. Mindful Tech Projects never sees your backups.
- **Autopilot (The Great Escape)** uses Claude's computer use on your own computer, only after you turn it on and give permission. You can stop it anytime with Esc.
- **Feedback** is something you choose to post, yourself, on this project's public GitHub page. The plugin drafts it with you but never sends it. Don't include anything private; posts there are public.
- **Payments** happen on Patreon, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or the crypto checkout, under their own privacy policies. The plugin never handles payments. Paying with crypto doesn't ask for card or bank details, but blockchain payments are public records, so it isn't anonymous.
- **The website** (freemynotes.com) is a plain page hosted on GitHub Pages, with no tracking of our own.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
