# Change summary — Escape from Evernote
*by Mindful Tech Projects*

Each version lists what changed and why, newest first.

---

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-26

**Theme: the first escape.** Built from a real move out of Evernote (476 notes, 32 notebooks, $22 a month saved), with every lesson from that move written into the plugin.

### Escape Hatch (free)
- **The escape plan:** a short interview (name, why you're leaving, work or personal, where your notes should go, phone or computer), then a nine-step plan you can stop and resume anytime.
- **Survey:** counts every notebook and note, pages through every result, and checks that the totals add up.
- **Sorting:** Work, Personal, Not sure, and Sensitive. The Work bucket can use your business name. Notebooks first, then the mixed ones note by note.
- **Sensitive-note protection:** finds passwords, logins, account and card numbers, ID numbers, and private legal, medical, or family notes. Asks what to do with each one (one at a time, by group, or all the same), keeps them out of Claude Projects and memory, offers a printable sheet, and deletes its own copies after you've moved them somewhere safe, only when you confirm.
- **Claude-ready files:** one Markdown file per notebook, in a folder per bucket, with a tracker and a "START HERE" guide for adding them to a Claude Project.
- **Backup and cancel:** step-by-step `.enex` export from the Evernote desktop app, a backup check against the survey, the cancel guide for Evernote, Apple, and Google Play billing, and a savings counter.
- **Progress log:** asks first, then remembers notebook names, counts, choices, and finished steps, never note contents or passwords. "What do you know about me" and "forget my escape" work anytime.
- **Voice-friendly mode:** short spoken updates while you drive; anything that needs a screen goes on an "at your computer" list.
- **Help and feedback:** fixes for the common problems (connector not connected, notes that look missing, attachments, backups), and "send feedback" drafts a note to the creator.

### Paid versions (one-time purchase)
- **Side Door ($9):** reads your `.enex` backup to get every PDF, photo, and attachment; copies to Google Drive with one Google Doc per note, a tracker sheet, and restricted folders.
- **Getaway Car ($18):** Notion, Word files and OneDrive, Markdown and Obsidian, and your own sorting buckets.
- **The Great Escape ($27, early access):** Autopilot (Claude does the export and the moving on your computer while you talk from your phone, with a guided setup), Apple Notes, Google Keep, and a beta hand-off to All of my Irons in the Fire!

### Also new

- Plugin marketplace, so the free version installs straight from this repository.
- Privacy note, help page, and feedback form.
