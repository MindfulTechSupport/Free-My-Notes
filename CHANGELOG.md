# Change summary — Free My Notes
*by Mindful Tech Projects*

Each version lists what changed and why, newest first.

---

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-27

**Theme: free your notes, from any app.** Built from a real move out of a paid notes app (476 notes, 32 notebooks, $22 a month saved), with every lesson from that move written into the plugin, then widened to the notes apps people actually use.

### Escape Hatch (free)
- **Fourteen notes apps from day one:** Evernote, Google Keep, Microsoft OneNote, Apple Notes, Notion, Zoho Notebook, Joplin, Simplenote, Bear, Standard Notes, Obsidian, UpNote, MyScript Notes (Nebo), and Zoom notes, plus a general path for others. Each has a guide: how Claude gets the notes, how to make a full backup, what's in it, and how to cancel.
- **The plan:** a short interview that starts with "Which notes app do you want your notes out of?" and "Leaving, or just a backup?", then a nine-step plan you can stop and resume anytime.
- **Survey:** counts every notebook (or label, tag, folder, or section) and note, through a connector or the app's export, and checks that the totals add up.
- **Sorting:** Work, Personal, Not sure, and Sensitive. The Work bucket can use your business name.
- **Sensitive-note protection:** finds passwords, logins, account and card numbers, ID numbers, and private legal, medical, or family notes. Asks what to do with each one, keeps them out of Claude Projects and memory, offers a printable sheet, and deletes its own copies after you've moved them somewhere safe, only when you confirm.
- **Claude-ready files:** one Markdown file per notebook, in a folder per bucket, with a tracker and a "START HERE" guide for adding them to a Claude Project.
- **Backup and cancel:** each app's full export, a backup check against the survey, where-you-pay cancel steps, and a savings counter.
- **Progress log:** asks first, then remembers which app, notebook names, counts, choices, and finished steps, never note contents or passwords.
- **Voice-friendly mode,** help, and "send feedback."

### Paid versions (one-time purchase)
- **Side Door ($9):** reads any app's backup in one pass, attachments included (Evernote .enex, Google Keep Takeout, Simplenote, Joplin JEX, Standard Notes, Notion, Obsidian, Bear, UpNote, Zoho Notebook, MyScript, Zoom, OneNote, and Apple Notes export apps); Google Drive with one Google Doc per note, a tracker sheet, and restricted folders.
- **Getaway Car ($18):** Notion, Word files and OneDrive, Markdown and Obsidian, and your own sorting buckets.
- **The Great Escape ($27, early access):** Autopilot (Claude makes the exports and does the moving on your computer while you talk from your phone, with a guided setup), Apple Notes and Google Keep as destinations from any app, and a beta hand-off to All of my Irons in the Fire!

### Backup service ($1 a month)
- A backup of your notes app every month, made in your own Claude and saved to your own Google Drive, with a check, a short report, and the last 3 backups kept. Automatic for Evernote, Notion, and Zoom; a reminder and a check for other apps.

### Also new
- Website at freemynotes.com with every version and every way to pay, including crypto with no card or bank details needed.
- Plugin marketplace, so the free version installs straight from this repository.
- Privacy note, help page, and feedback form.
